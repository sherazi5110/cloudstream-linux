## What's new in 7.0.0

This release has the **Linux** app (`.deb`, `.flatpak`, `.tar`) and the **Android** app (`.apk` for phones, tablets,
Android TV and Fire TV). The new features are on both.

- **Downloads for watching offline.** Use *Download* on a movie, the download button on an episode, or *Download these*
  for a whole season on Linux. Direct files and HLS streams are supported: HLS segments are joined into one file and
  AES-128 is decrypted. Subtitles are saved next to the video. Downloads resume after a pause, a restart or a dropped
  connection. A downloaded episode plays from the file, also offline, and your progress is shared with streaming.
  Find them in Library → **Downloads**.
  - Linux saves them to `~/Downloads/CloudStream`; you can change the folder in Settings → Storage.
  - Android keeps them in the app's own folder, with a progress notification (*Pause all*). Uninstalling the app
    deletes them.
- **Skip intro (anime).** *Skip intro*, *Skip recap* and *Skip credits* buttons use times from the community AniSkip
  database, or the app can skip them automatically (Settings → Playback).
- **Next-episode countdown.** From the credits, or 20 seconds before the end, a card counts down to the next episode,
  with *Play now* and *Cancel*.
- **TV guide grid for Live TV.** Channels run down the side and times across the top, with a line at "now". Click a
  programme to watch that channel. Open it with *TV guide* in the Live TV top bar on Linux, or the clock button on
  Android.
- **"Wrong match?"** If a title page shows ratings or artwork from a different film, search TMDB and pick the right
  one. You can also choose *No match* or go back to *Automatic*.
- The **Flatpak** now runs on the Freedesktop **25.08** runtime. Flathub must be set up (see below); it downloads the
  runtime once.
- Fixed: a moved-site address (Settings → Site addresses) could restore the wrong original address after an extension
  update.

Can't download: DASH-only streams, torrents, live channels and DRM-protected streams.
